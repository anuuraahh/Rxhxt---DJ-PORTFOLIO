    Story

    From a carnival stall to Red Bull.

    I started out in Soundation, a browser studio, then moved to FL Studio, and today I produce for artists. I make the sounds people don't often hear, and the kind of music people remember me by.

    I started DJing in high school, in 2024, on a school-carnival DJ floor I ran with friends. Ideas for mixes and transitions kept coming to me while producing, so I moved to Virtual DJ. Then came a Halloween party at Medai The Stage, and Red Bull FOUR 2 SCORE, where everything changed. The best part of any set is the look on people's faces.

    Dhwani

    Fifteen people. One sound.

    I co-founded Dhwani, a 15-member band that plays live across Coimbatore.

    Every gig is built for the room we're playing. We put the set together as a band, rehearse it until it locks in, and play it fully live, from intimate cafe nights to college stages. With fifteen of us up there, it never sounds like a cover. It sounds like a crowd singing back.
