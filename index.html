    Story

    From a carnival stall to Red Bull.

    I started making music at 13, on Christmas, for a Christmas video. I make the sounds people don't often hear, and the kind of music people remember me by.

    That first song was made in Soundation, a browser studio. Then came FL Studio, and today I produce for artists across Coimbatore.

    I started DJing in high school, in 2024, on a school-carnival DJ floor I ran with friends. Ideas for mixes and transitions kept coming to me while producing, so I moved to Virtual DJ. Then came a Halloween party at Medai The Stage, and Red Bull's FOUR 2 SCORE, where everything changed. The best part of any set is the look on people's faces.
